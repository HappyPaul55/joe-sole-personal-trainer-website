About

Who is Joe Sole?

I'm a certified personal trainer who thinks the gym should be the friendliest part of your week. I've spent a decade helping people of every age — from first-timers nervous about the squat rack to lifters chasing big PRs — find a version of fitness they actually enjoy.

My coaching is simple: listen first, program smart, and celebrate every win — from your first push-up to your first deadlift PR. No intimidation, no ego. Just honest coaching and steady, sustainable progress.
